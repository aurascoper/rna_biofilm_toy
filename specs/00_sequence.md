Sequence and gates for everything after the toy. Status: record only. Nothing on this page is code, and nothing on it starts before the toy has shown one measured result with a prediction committed first (PREDICTION.md) and before Biofilms PRs #72 and #73 and the manuscript revision are done.

Two decisions were hiding in "3-D CPM" and they are separate. Dimension: the replicator-and-parasite results the toy paraphrases (Boerlijst and Hogeweg's hypercycles, Szabo and colleagues 2002 on limited dispersal selecting for efficiency and fidelity, Takeuchi and Hogeweg after them) are 2-D cellular automata, and spatial structure protects cooperators because a parasite can only spread along a front; in 3-D a site has 26 neighbours, fronts are thicker, and the protection weakens. Whether the 2-D result survives in 3-D is a real question and the right second experiment, but it is an experiment on a 2-D result that does not exist yet. Formalism: a cellular Potts model models things with extent (volume constraint, adhesion, a Hamiltonian, Metropolis acceptance); a ribozyme has none of that, so for molecules in a matrix the CA is correct and a CPM is ceremony. The CPM earns its place when the entity has a shape, which means a protocell, and then it is written from the Hamiltonian in about 300 lines in its own repository, not forked from Biofilms, whose code is welded to its evidence discipline, checkpoint schema and claims tests. Electrostatics is added only when a genotype variable pays a cost or gains a yield from it; a potential nothing reads is the same inert passenger as a nutrient field nothing consumed. Rendering is solved: any field is one more array in a .vti and the existing viewer reads it. The molecular lane (03) is independent of all of this.

Two triage pages sit beside the lanes: specs/06 maps the chapters of classical electrodynamics to what, if anything, reads them at each lane's pitch and timescale, and specs/07 does the same for evolutionary game theory, decision theory and quorum sensing. So the order is: fixed 2-D toy to one result with a prediction first; 3-D CA to test whether it survives; CPM only when a protocell appears; electrostatics when something reads it; rendering through the existing pipeline.

Three further records, so they survive the conversation that produced them.

First, pitch and energy are coupled, and this is a gate on any lane that carries a dose field. Once the pitch and the attenuation or range coefficient are both declared, the gradient across the domain is determined, not chosen, and a prediction about depth is vacuous if the field is flat across the lattice. The worked case is the shipped Biofilms field I(r) = I0 exp(-kappa r / N) with kappa = 2 and N = 40, which is mu = 0.05 per site and an axis-to-wall ratio of e, 2.72, over R = 20 sites. Ask what photon energy that is and the pitch falls out. Using mass attenuation coefficients for water recalled from NIST XCOM (0.0863 cm2/g at 662 keV, 0.0632 at 1.25 MeV, 0.137 at 208 keV; all to be verified against XCOM before the note below is written), the implied pitch is about 5.8 mm per site for a 662 keV photon (a 23 cm domain) and about 7.9 mm per site for 1.25 MeV (32 cm). Run the other way, at 1 um per site, the axis-to-wall ratio for MeV gamma is 1.0001: no gradient. Only photons near 1 keV give a 2.7 ratio across 40 um, and those are stopped in microns and are not what a reactor emits. So the shipped gradient is quantitatively consistent with reactor-relevant gamma at pool scale, tens of centimetres, and is not realizable at biofilm scale at any plausible energy. This does not contradict the earlier null result, which was about the magnitude of beta_ion; it is a separate statement. It also says where structure at biofilm-to-millimetre scale comes from: beta emitters, whose endpoint ranges are millimetres in water, not gamma; a beta emitter with a few-hundred-keV endpoint and 100 to 200 keV gamma lines gives a gradient through its betas and none through its gammas at that scale.

Two further diagnostics are owed to the Biofilms manuscript and sit, as spec pages, in ~/Downloads/spec_symbolics_delta_h_and_continuum.md and ~/Downloads/spec_turing_identifiability_ridge.md (both dated 2026-10-06, both spec only, both gated behind the revision and PRs #72 and #73; neither changes a simulation file). The Symbolics page carries two findings whose arithmetic was re-checked here. The volume term expands exactly: (V plus or minus 1 minus V_target) squared minus (V minus V_target) squared equals plus or minus 2(V minus V_target) plus 1, so at V = V_target both growth and shrinkage cost +lambda_V = 10, and with T_cpm = 5 the volume term alone caps Metropolis acceptance of any volume-changing move at exp(-2) = 0.135 before any other term is consulted; whether the manuscript states that floor, and whether the reported acceptance rates are consistent with it, is a grep that has not been done. The 6-point stencil is Delta-x squared times the Laplacian plus O(Delta-x to the fourth), carrying no 1/Delta-x squared, so D_C = 0.2 has units of lattice-length squared per field step and the physical diffusivity D_C times Delta-x squared over Delta-t is undeclared until the pitch is; declaring a pitch constrains more than the attenuation. The Turing page turns the Appendix A parameter-counting argument into a posterior: from D10 = 838.1 plus or minus 98.0 Gy, single-hit gives alpha = ln10/838.1 = 0.0027474 per Gy exactly, while linear-quadratic gives a line of solutions from (alpha, beta) = (0, 3.278e-6) to (0.0027474, 0), every point reproducing S(D10) = 0.1; a synthetic second point at 200 Gy with S = 0.6 collapses it to alpha = 0.0024936, beta = 3.03e-7; the likelihood belongs on the dose, not on survival, and NUTS divergences on the one-point case are the ridge's signature rather than a tuning failure. On the four Julia packages considered: Turing first, because the identifiability question is Bayesian in other clothes; Symbolics second, with the caution that simplify is not canonical in any CAS so simplify(a) == simplify(b) is unsound and the sound form is iszero(simplify(a - b)) plus random numeric substitution; Metal is the wrong tool for this model (Apple GPUs have no double precision, Metropolis is sequential by construction, and the field solves measured at 189 ms per 100-MCS run at N = 40 are a few hundred kernel launches of work); Flux is deferred until there is a question it answers, since an emulator discussed as model behaviour is White 2014 again.

Third, two lanes outrank 01 to 03 on the test that should decide priority, which is what measurement the model would be checked against, and neither has a spec page here. Melanin attenuation can be checked against the ISS Cladosporium sphaerospermum result and Dadachova's D10 values, and melanin is already in the Biofilms model with literature behind it. Biosorption of research-reactor environmentals (activation and fission products: tritium in pool water, Co-60 and the other activated-steel nuclides, Cs-137, Ar-41 airborne; not actinides) by biofilms, including melanized fungi documented in spent-fuel pools, is the same question as the bench assay already being scoped, which is the lane with a measurement at the end of it. Ribozymes entered this work from the pasted documents, not from anything built here, and have no measurement available. They were ranked and deferred, not forgotten. One caution goes with this record: facility specifics must come from public licensing and environmental monitoring reports, not from memory. Beta emitters are where a dose field gets structure at biofilm-to-millimetre scale, and a beta-emitter dose model already exists elsewhere in this family of work.
