Protocell cellular Potts model. Status: spec only, no code. Gate: starts only after the 2-D toy has one measured result with a prediction committed first, and after Biofilms PRs #72 and #73 and the manuscript revision. Its own repository when it starts; nothing forked from Biofilms.

Pitch: 1 um per site, declared here on line one. Reason: at seawater or vent ionic strength the Debye length is about a nanometre, so at micrometre pitch a bulk potential is screened to nothing within a small fraction of one site, and a volumetric Poisson solve would compute a field that is zero everywhere except at interfaces. What survives at this pitch is the interface potential: the Nernst or Donnan drop across a mineral wall or a protocell membrane, about 59 mV per decade of proton ratio, so roughly 180 mV across the three pH units of the alkaline-vent scenario (Russell, Martin, Lane). That is a boundary quantity, not a bulk field. At nanometre pitch one gets real bulk electrostatics on a domain a few hundred nanometres across with no room for an ecosystem; the two regimes do not meet in the middle, and this lane lives in the micrometre one.

Entity: a fatty-acid vesicle or protocell, which is what gives the CPM its reason to exist here. It occupies many sites, has a target volume, an adhesion energy with other parcels and with the wall, and moves by Metropolis acceptance of single-site copy attempts. The Hamiltonian is adhesion plus volume constraint, written from memory, about 300 lines. A ribozyme would have none of that, which is why the ribozyme lanes are cellular automata.

Electrostatics, when and only when a genotype variable pays a cost or gains a yield from it. The mathematical shape already exists in Biofilms: the radiation term delta H = plus or minus beta_ion times I_local is exactly q times delta phi with a static field playing the potential. Electrostatics here is that term with the potential made self-consistent: charges on sites give rho, a Poisson solve on the same six-point Laplacian the diffusion uses gives phi, and a mover pays q times delta phi. Ions, if wanted, are the nutrient update plus a drift term, Nernst-Planck. Permittivity, mobilities and the wall potential are declared values with provenance (published value, units, source, date checked), never round numbers, in the same table format as spec 01. Any radiation term later added at this pitch must come from a short-range emitter (alpha, beta, fission fragment) or be declared uniform, for the same reason this page declares its pitch against the Debye length: at micrometre pitch a gamma field has no gradient across the domain (see specs/00_sequence.md).

Rendering: every field is one more array in a .vti written with the pitch declared (the Biofilms writer refuses a spacing other than 1 without a declared pitch, and that is the right behaviour to copy), read by the existing viewer. No new tool.

Open before any code: which genotype variable reads the potential, and what it costs. Without an answer the electrostatics stays out.
