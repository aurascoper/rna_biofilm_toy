Electrodynamics triage: which chapters of classical electrodynamics anything in this project can read. Status: triage only, no code, nothing gated on it. The rule applied to every row is the one the thread has used throughout: a field or term that nothing in a model reads is an inert passenger with an impressive name, so a chapter earns a place only when a genotype variable or a measured observable reads a quantity it supplies, with declared units and a provenance row. Every verdict below is conditional on the pitch and timescale named in its row; a change to either voids the row, and each "none" names what would reopen it. The four readers are the 2-D toy (50 by 50 cylinder, pitch undeclared, one step per update), the protocell CPM of spec 02 (1 um per site, ionic timescales of milliseconds), the molecular lane of spec 03 (angstrom pitch, albumin in electrolyte), and the dose physics behind spec 01's feasibility table. The shipped Biofilms radiation field and the dose arithmetic already live in specs 00 and 01 and are not repeated here. Isotopes are not named on this page; rows are stated for electrons and alphas of given energy in water, with the energy each number belongs to stated beside it.

Regime in one paragraph. A vent pore or a biofilm at micrometre pitch and millisecond timescales is electro-quasistatic. The magnetic terms scale as (v/c) squared against the electric ones, which for ions drifting at 1 mm/s is 1.1e-23. The good-conductor skin depth in seawater (sigma 5 S/m) is 7.1 m at 1 kHz and 0.23 m at 1 MHz, where sigma/(omega epsilon) is 1.1e6 and 1.1e3 and the formula holds; at 1 GHz that ratio is 1.1, seawater is a transition medium, and the good-conductor formula no longer applies, which is the number at which propagation would reopen. The free-space wavelength at 1 kHz is 300 km. Bulk electrostatics is screened within a Debye length, 3.0 nm at 0.01 M, 0.96 nm at 0.1 M, 0.43 nm at 0.5 M, 0.36 nm at 0.7 M, so at 1 um per site a bulk potential is zero everywhere except at interfaces and what survives is Coulomb plus Nernst at walls and membranes, 59.2 mV per decade of ion ratio at 298 K.

What this book does not supply, so the triage cannot be read as complete coverage of the dose field: photon attenuation coefficients (Compton and photoelectric, quantum processes; the XCOM values in spec 01; Jackson gives Thomson scattering, the classical low-energy limit, which is not what those tables contain); beta spectrum shapes and mean energies (Fermi theory; LNHB/DDEP tables); nuclear decay data generally (branch intensities, half-lives; LNHB). Spec 01 takes mean energies from LNHB and does not derive them.

Order in which the rows with a job would be taken up, if any are: units first, because it is free and fixes the four quantities named; then a hand reproduction of one ASTAR row (5.5 MeV alpha in water) from the Bethe formula within a stated tolerance, as a check on the feasibility table; then Beer-Lambert for a declared light source, with scattering as its correction; then electrostatics through specs 02 and 03 as already gated. Cherenkov is recorded as a real, small photon source for pool-scale lanes and parked with the melanin lane until an activity and a geometry are declared. No term enters a model until a genotype variable or a measured observable reads it.
