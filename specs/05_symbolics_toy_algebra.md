S1, the selection coefficient. With k = 0.25, own purine u, neighbours' purine sum S, local flux F and decay constant c, damage per step is F exp(-k u S) c and the survival time of an organism that never replicates is t = exp(k u S)/(F c). Derive d(ln t)/du = k S exactly, independent of flux. Expected values, computed here and to be written into the file as the test: with neighbours at purine 1.5, the survival-time gain for one +0.05 purine mutation is 0.0 percent at 0 neighbours (S = 0), 3.8 percent at 2 (k S = 0.75), 7.8 percent at 4 (k S = 1.5), 16.2 percent at 8 (k S = 3.0). Correction 2026-10-07: this derivative quantifies a fixed-neighborhood, nonreplicating survival-time sensitivity. Mutation-supply arithmetic can explain limited genotype movement, but does not isolate its population-level cause or establish that mutation rate is the only effective lever. At S=0 this assumed shielding law supplies no private purine protection; clustering and genotype effects require controlled comparisons. Control: perturb k in one of the two sides and require both detectors to fail.

S2, the index law. For an interior strip of height h spanning all WIDTH columns on the cylinder, derive mean occupied degree = 8 - 6/h, mean available degree = 8, N = WIDTH h, and hence clustering_index = (8 - 6/h) (WIDTH HEIGHT - 1) / (8 (N - 1)), which falls like 2499/N at fixed shape; for a strip touching an edge row the available degree is 8 - 3/h instead. Expected values measured here for h = 3, 6, 10, 20 (N = 150, 300, 500, 1000): index 12.58, 7.31, 4.63, 2.41. This is the confound that makes an unmatched comparison between arms manufacture the predicted sign, and tests/test_engine.py pins it numerically; this page gives it a formula. Control: drop the -6/h term and require both detectors to fail.

S3, the ledger identity and the monomer steady state. Prove symbolically that M(t) - M(t - 1) = injected_gross - inject_clip - diffusion_clip - birth_sink + death_return holds for one site's update composed of injection with clamp, a conservative transfer, a birth deduction and a clamped death return, so the identity the runner asserts numerically every step is a theorem rather than an observation. Then solve the steady state of the vent disk under constant injection against the replication sink, treating the colony as a uniform consumer at the replication threshold, to answer whether the vent can sustain the population at all, a question the toy raises and never asks. Control: flip the sign of birth_sink and require both detectors to fail.
