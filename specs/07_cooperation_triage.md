Cooperation triage: what in this project reads evolutionary game theory, decision theory and quorum sensing. Status: triage only, no code, nothing gated on it. Same rule as specs/06: a body of theory earns a place only when a genotype variable or a measured observable reads a quantity it supplies, with declared units and a provenance row; every verdict is conditional on the model it names and says what would reopen a none. The readers are the 2-D toy as committed, the protocell CPM of spec 02, and the discipline itself (PREDICTION.md, spec 04, and the Biofilms rules the specs mirror). Citations at the foot were verified against Crossref on 2026-10-07 and carry their DOIs.

Evolutionary game theory. Work the toy's structure out before naming it. Each organism's damage is flux times exp(-k u_i S_i) times decay, where S_i is the sum of its neighbours' purine. Raising your own purine u_i benefits you by k S_i (the private half, which spec 05 derives as d(ln survival time)/du), and it also benefits each neighbour j by k u_j, because your u_i sits inside their S_j (the public half, of the same order and previously unmentioned). Both directions are benefits and nothing anywhere is a cost. That is a pure mutualism: there is no defection strategy, so there is no dilemma, and Hamilton's rule, spatial reciprocity and the public-goods results are all about dilemmas. The accurate sentence is that the toy has the benefit structure of a public good and no cost, so it is not a social dilemma; adding a cost is the engine change that would make it one, and it needs its own pre-registration. Selection on purine as the toy stands is the sum of both halves, frozen by mutation supply. The replicator-and-parasite question the thread named as the toy's first measured result (parasite rescue by restricted dispersal) is the spatial-games literature in its original form: hypercycles stable against parasites on a lattice, limited dispersal selecting for efficiency and fidelity, the review of RNA-like replicator systems, and the spatial prisoner's dilemma that made the general point. Who reads it: a second genotype variable, catalytic contribution to neighbours' replication with a declared cost, which does not exist in the toy and is out of scope until PREDICTION.md stage 2 has run. Verdict: has a job as the first experiment after the pre-registered result, under its own prediction; none in the toy as committed. Declare first: the benefit a contributor gives a neighbour (replication help per step), its cost (monomers or survival), dispersal distance (the toy fixes it at one site; the literature's lever is varying it), and the payoff's dependence on neighbour count, since on a cylinder the two edge rows have five neighbours and the rest eight, which the clustering index already has to account for. What would reopen the none: adding the cost term; with a cost, the k S benefit and the cost together define the game, and the lattice determines whether cooperators survive, which is the result that would be measured.

Decision theory. It has a job already and is not a model term. The two-stage pre-registration in PREDICTION.md is a decision rule fixed before the data: floors at twice the null-arm seed-to-seed spread, declared and committed before the arms run. The Biofilms rule the specs mirror, that delta stays unset and must never be chosen from any effect measured in the repository, is the same thing stated as a prohibition: a threshold selected from observed data certifies the noise that produced it. Spec 04 supplies posteriors for the magnitude of each arm contrast; turning a posterior into a decision needs a loss, and this project has not declared one because the toy is a sandbox from which no decision is made. Who reads it: the person writing the prediction line and the floors. Verdict: has a job as discipline; none as a term in any model. Declare first, if a decision is ever to be made from a run: the loss for calling a direction wrong versus calling a magnitude wrong, and the prior over effect sizes, in the same file as the floors. One organism-level reading exists and belongs to the CPM, not the toy: bet-hedging, where a lineage pays a growth cost for phenotypic diversity in a fluctuating environment; it needs an environment that fluctuates and a switching trait, neither of which the toy has. What would reopen the none: a declared fluctuation in the field or the vent and a switching genotype variable in spec 02.

Quorum sensing. In bacteria it is density-dependent regulation through a secreted, diffusing autoinducer that accumulates until a threshold concentration switches gene expression; the biofilm literature models it as a second diffusing field produced by cells and read by a trait. Two cautions from that literature are controls in the sense this project uses: the signal can report diffusion limitation rather than cell number (diffusion sensing), or the efficiency of secreting anything at all (efficiency sensing), so a model in which a trait reads a signal field has to say which of those it is measuring, and a result that cannot distinguish them is a check that cannot fail. In the toy there is no signal field; the clustering index and the shielding sum S are local density read directly, with no secreted intermediate, no production rate, no decay and no threshold, which is the honest description of what the toy has: density dependence without sensing. An RNA-world replicator has no gene expression to regulate, so quorum sensing proper is a protocell-era question at the earliest. Who reads it: in spec 02, a trait switched by a declared autoinducer field, implemented as the monomer update (the same conservative diffusion, which the toy already has) plus a production term from occupied sites and a first-order decay, both of which need units and therefore a pitch and a seconds-per-step. Verdict: none in the toy; has a job in the CPM lane only when a trait reads a declared signal field. Declare first: production rate per organism, decay rate, diffusion coefficient in physical units (hence pitch and time step), the threshold, and which of cell density, diffusion limitation or secretion efficiency the signal is claimed to measure, with the control that distinguishes them.

Order, if any of this is taken up: the cost term for purine, because it turns the mutualism the toy already contains into a dilemma and makes the parasite-rescue result the natural first pre-registered experiment after stage 2; then nothing from quorum sensing until spec 02 exists; decision theory is already in force through the floors and needs no code.
